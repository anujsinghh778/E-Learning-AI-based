Our Heritage

The Legend of Nirmal Chand's Brass Scale

In the autumn of 1972, Shri Nirmal Chand opened a tiny spice corner in the heart of old Jodhpur. Armed with only a manual brass scale and a commitment to never adulterate his goods, he won the trust of local families one ounce at a time.

Unlike modern high-speed industrial factories that burn away spice oils, we grind our spices slowly. We still weigh and pack every order by hand, ensuring that when the package is opened in your kitchen, the aroma takes you straight to the spice markets of Marwar.
